Maxim

A few causes drive most results (the 80/20 rule).

Effort and outcome are rarely in proportion. Find the vital few things that matter and give them your best attention.

E = ln(1+Q) + √K + 3·T + 1.5·R
Moral Rank = 100 × E / max E
  • Quote weight w = verification (verified 1.0, probable 0.6, disputed 0.2, unverified 0, misattributed 0) × relation (expresses 1.0, similar 0.7, partial 0.4, contrasts 0) × our confidence that the quote fits the maxim.
  • Q is the sum of all quote weights. It sits under a logarithm, so each extra quote adds a little less than the last. Q = 0.48 → ln(1+Q) = 0.39
  • K counts each thinker once, at their strongest quote, under a square root: many voices matter, but with diminishing returns. K = 0.48 → √K = 0.69
  • T adds up the distinct traditions, each counted at its strongest quote, with weight 3. A tradition that grew out of another tradition already supporting the maxim (for example Christianity from Judaism) counts half (0.5). T = 0.48 → 3·T = 1.44

  • Moral Rank divides E by the highest E on the site and scales to 100, so the best-supported maxim scores 100. Concepts are not linked to one another yet, so nothing propagates between them. E = 4.02, max E = 13.66 → 29.5
