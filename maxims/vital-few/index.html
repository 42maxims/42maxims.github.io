A few causes drive most results (the 80/20 rule).

Effort and outcome are rarely in proportion. Find the vital few things that matter and give them your best attention.

Voices

“For many outcomes, roughly 80% of consequences come from 20% of causes.”
— Joseph M. Juran (after Vilfredo Pareto), Pareto principle, as summarised by Wikipedia (paraphrase, not a direct quote)
