Maxim

A few causes drive most results (the 80/20 rule).

Effort and outcome are rarely in proportion. Find the vital few things that matter and give them your best attention.

On hold for The 42: The older quotes here (Marcus Aurelius, Luke 10) echo only the 'focus on what matters' half of this idea, not the claim that a few causes drive most results, so it waits in Emerging until a closer independent source is found.
