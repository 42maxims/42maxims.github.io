Maxim

Do what is just, even when it costs you.

Justice asks us to be fair when fairness is inconvenient: to our rivals, to strangers, and against our own interest. Many traditions hold that it is better to suffer a wrong than to do one, and that a fair heart is worth more than any advantage.
