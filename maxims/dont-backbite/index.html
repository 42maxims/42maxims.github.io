Maxim

Don't speak ill of people behind their backs.

Talk about someone who is not there to answer can quietly wound them and divide friends. Many traditions single out backbiting and talebearing as a wrong of their own: what we would not say to a person's face, or what was told us in confidence, is better left unsaid.

Themes: Speech: honesty, gossip and listening
