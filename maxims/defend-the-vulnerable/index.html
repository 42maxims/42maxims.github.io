Maxim

Defend the widow, the orphan and the poor.

Those without a protector are the easiest to overlook and to wrong. From the law codes of Babylon and the instructions of Egyptian kings to the Hebrew prophets, the Qur'an and Mencius, the measure of a just person or ruler is how they treat the widow, the fatherless and the needy, and whether they speak up for them.

Themes: Justice and fairness
