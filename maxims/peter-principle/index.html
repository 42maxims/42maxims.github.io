People rise to their level of incompetence.

A gently humbling observation about organisations: we tend to be promoted until we land in a role we no longer do well. Know your strengths.

Voices

“In a hierarchy, every employee tends to rise to his level of incompetence.”
— Laurence J. Peter, The Peter Principle (1969); checked via Wikipedia, not the book
