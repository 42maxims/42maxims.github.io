Craving is the root of suffering.

Much of our pain comes not from what happens, but from our grasping for things to be otherwise. Loosen the grip and the weight lightens.

Voices

“The noble truth of the origination of stress: the craving that makes for further becoming.”
— The Buddha, Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta (SN 56.11), tr. Thanissaro Bhikkhu
