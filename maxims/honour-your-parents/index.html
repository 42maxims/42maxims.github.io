Maxim

Honour your father and your mother.

The people who raised us gave us our first world, however imperfectly. Many traditions ask us to meet them with care and respect, not just duty, and to let that gratitude show in how we treat them as they grow older.

Related expressions

  • “The filial piety of now-a-days means the support of one's parents. But dogs and horses likewise are able to do something in the way of support;-- without reverence, what is there to distinguish the one support given from the other?”
    — Confucius, Analects 2.7 (Wei Zheng), tr. James Legge
