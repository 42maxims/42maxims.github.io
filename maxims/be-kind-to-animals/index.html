Maxim

Be kind to animals.

Animals feel hunger, fear and pain, and many of them live in our care or at our mercy. Traditions far apart treat the way we treat them as a quiet measure of our character, and kindness to a thirsty creature as a good deed in its own right.

Themes: Kindness and compassion
