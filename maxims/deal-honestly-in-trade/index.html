Maxim

Give full measure: deal honestly in trade.

Fair weights and honest measures are among the oldest moral rules we know. Cheating in small ways, a short measure here or a fake mixed in with the genuine there, wears away the trust that every exchange depends on.

Themes: Justice and fairness
