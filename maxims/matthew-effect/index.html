Maxim

Advantage tends to compound: those who have, get more.

Early advantages (a head start, a good name, a little capital) tend to attract more of the same, while those with little often find even that slipping away. The Gospel of Matthew states the pattern twice, and in 1968 the sociologists Robert K. Merton and Harriet Zuckerman named it the Matthew effect. Seeing it clearly invites us to share a head start generously and to lift up those who begin behind.
