Maxim

Seek peace, and pursue it.

Peace rarely arrives by itself; it is something we go after, in quarrels at home as much as between nations. To be the one who lowers the temperature, and who takes the offered hand, is honoured in nearly every tradition.

Themes: Anger, conflict and forgiveness
