Maxim

When you fall, get back up.

Falling is not the failure; staying down is. Traditions far apart in time and place honour the person who stumbles, gathers themselves, and rises again, as many times as it takes.

Related expressions

  • “Our greatest glory is, not in never falling; but in rising every time we fall.”
    — Oliver Goldsmith (often misattributed to Confucius), The Citizen of the World (1762), vol. 1, Letter VII. Spoken by Goldsmith's fictional Chinese narrator, who credits Confucius; the line is not found in Confucius's works (see Quote Investigator, https://quoteinvestigator.com/2014/05/27/rising/).
