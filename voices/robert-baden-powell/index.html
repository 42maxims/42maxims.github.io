Robert Baden-Powell

1857–1941Scouting movementModern (1800 – 1950)Person

Robert Baden-Powell was a British Army officer who founded the Scout movement; his handbook Scouting for Boys appeared in 1908. He served as Chief Scout of the World until his death in Kenya in 1941.
