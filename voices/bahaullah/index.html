Voice

Bahá’u’lláh

1817–1892Bahá’í FaithModern (1800 – 1950)Person

Bahá’u’lláh, born Mírzá Ḥusayn-ʻAlí Núrí in Tehran, founded the Bahá’í Faith. Exiled from Persia and imprisoned by the Ottoman authorities, he wrote extensively, and The Hidden Words, composed in Baghdad around 1858, is one of his best-known works.
