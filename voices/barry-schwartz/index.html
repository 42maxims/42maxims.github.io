Barry Schwartz

born 1946Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Barry Schwartz is an American psychologist who taught for many years at Swarthmore College. In The Paradox of Choice (2004) he argues that having too many options can leave people less satisfied.
