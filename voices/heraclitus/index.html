Heraclitus

fl. c. 500 BCEGreek philosophyAncient (before 200 BCE)Person

Heraclitus of Ephesus was a Greek philosopher who taught that the world is always changing, held together by an underlying order he called the logos. His book is lost and survives only in fragments quoted by later writers such as Plutarch and Clement of Alexandria.
