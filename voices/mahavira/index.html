Voice

Mahavira

traditionally 599–527 BCE; many scholars place him in the 5th century BCEJainismAncient (before 200 BCE)Person

Mahavira, born Vardhamana, is revered in Jainism as the twenty-fourth and last tirthankara of the present age. A contemporary of the Buddha, he taught non-violence (ahimsa) and self-discipline, and his teachings were passed on by his disciples in the Jain scriptures.
