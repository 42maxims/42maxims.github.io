Voice

Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn

1918–2008Russian literature & thoughtContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn was a Russian novelist and historian. Arrested in 1945 while serving as a Red Army officer for criticising Stalin in private letters, he spent eight years in labour camps and later wrote about them in One Day in the Life of Ivan Denisovich (1962) and The Gulag Archipelago. He received the Nobel Prize in Literature in 1970, was expelled from the Soviet Union in 1974, and returned to Russia in 1994.
