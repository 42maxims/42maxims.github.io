Paul the Apostle

c. 5 – c. 64/65 CEChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Person

Paul of Tarsus was a Jew who joined the early followers of Jesus and became their most influential missionary to non-Jews. Thirteen New Testament letters bear his name: scholars widely accept seven as his own, while others, such as Ephesians, Colossians and the letters to Timothy, are traditionally attributed to him but disputed.
