Rabbis of the Talmud

c. 3rd–6th century CEJudaismClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Collective

The Babylonian Talmud records the debates, teachings and stories of generations of rabbis, building on the Mishnah. Many of its lessons come as stories, like that of Honi the Circle-Drawer and the man planting a carob tree.
