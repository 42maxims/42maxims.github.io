William Shakespeare

1564–1616English poetry & literatureEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Person

William Shakespeare was an English playwright and poet, born in Stratford-upon-Avon and active in London. His plays, among them The Merchant of Venice, written in the 1590s, are among the most performed and quoted in the English language.
