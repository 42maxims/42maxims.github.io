Epictetus

c. 50 – c. 135 CEStoicismClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Person

Epictetus was a Greek Stoic philosopher, born a slave in Hierapolis in Phrygia, who taught in Rome and later at Nicopolis in Greece. He wrote nothing himself; his student Arrian recorded his teaching in the Discourses and the short handbook known as the Enchiridion.
