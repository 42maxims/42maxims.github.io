Michel de Montaigne

1533–1592Renaissance humanismEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Person

Michel de Montaigne was a French writer of the Renaissance who, after retiring to his estate near Bordeaux, began writing short, searching pieces about himself and human nature. He called them essais, 'attempts', and so gave the essay its name; they were first published in 1580 and expanded in 1588. He also served as mayor of Bordeaux.
