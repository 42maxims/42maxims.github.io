Treatise on Action and Response

12th century (Song dynasty)TaoismMedieval (600 – 1500)Scripture

The Taishang Ganying Pian is an anonymous Daoist morality book that teaches that good and bad deeds bring due reward and punishment. It blends Daoist, Confucian and Buddhist ethics and became one of the most widely printed religious tracts in late imperial China.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
