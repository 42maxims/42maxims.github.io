Voice

The Qur'an

revealed c. 610–632 CE; standard text compiled c. 650 CEIslamMedieval (600 – 1500)Scripture

The Qur'an is the central scripture of Islam, believed by Muslims to be the word of God revealed to the Prophet Muhammad over some twenty-three years. According to Islamic tradition, its standard written text was compiled under the caliph Uthman around 650 CE.
