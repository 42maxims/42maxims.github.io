René Descartes

1596–1650RationalismEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Person

René Descartes was a French philosopher, mathematician and scientist, often called the father of modern philosophy. His Discourse on the Method (1637) sets out a way of reasoning from doubt to certainty, beginning from 'I think, therefore I am'.
