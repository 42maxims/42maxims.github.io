Voice

Tim Ferriss

born 1977Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Tim Ferriss is an American entrepreneur, investor, author and podcaster, host of The Tim Ferriss Show. He is known for his 4-Hour series of books, beginning with The 4-Hour Workweek (2007), and for his exercise of 'fear-setting', which he draws from Stoic writers such as Seneca.
