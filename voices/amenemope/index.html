Voice

Amenemope

text probably composed in the Ramesside period (c. 1300–1075 BCE)Ancient Egyptian wisdom literatureAncient (before 200 BCE)Traditional or legendary figure

The Instruction of Amenemope presents itself as the advice of the scribe Amenemope, son of Kanakht, to his son. Nothing is known of him outside the text. The work is best known for its close parallels with part of the biblical Book of Proverbs.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
