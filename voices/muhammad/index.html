The Prophet Muhammad

c. 570–632 CEIslamMedieval (600 – 1500)Person

Muhammad, born in Mecca, is revered by Muslims as the final prophet of God, to whom the Qur'an was revealed. Reports of his words and deeds (hadith) were passed on by his companions and later gathered into collections such as Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim.
