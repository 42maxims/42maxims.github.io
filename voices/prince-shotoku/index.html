Prince Shōtoku

574–622Early Japanese thought (Shōtoku)Medieval (600 – 1500)Person

Prince Shōtoku was regent of Japan under Empress Suiko and a great patron of Buddhism. The Nihongi (720) credits him with the Seventeen-Article Constitution of 604, which draws on Confucian and Buddhist ideals; some historians doubt that he wrote it himself.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
