Voice

James

1st century CEChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Person

The Letter of James opens in the name of 'James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ'. Tradition identifies him with James the brother of Jesus, a leader of the church in Jerusalem who died around 62 CE; many modern scholars think the letter was written later in his name.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
