Krishna

not historically datableHinduismAncient (before 200 BCE)Traditional or legendary figure

Krishna is revered in Hinduism as a deity, an avatar of Vishnu and, in many traditions, the Supreme Being. In the Bhagavad Gita, part of the epic Mahabharata, he speaks as charioteer and teacher to the warrior Arjuna on the eve of battle.
