Hippocrates

c. 460 – c. 370 BCEHippocratic medicineAncient (before 200 BCE)Person

Hippocrates of Kos was a Greek physician, often called the father of medicine. The Hippocratic Corpus, some sixty early medical writings, bears his name, though scholars think most were written by different authors of his school.
