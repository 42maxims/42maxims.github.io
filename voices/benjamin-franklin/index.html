Benjamin Franklin

1706–1790Enlightenment thoughtEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Person

Benjamin Franklin was an American printer, writer, scientist and statesman, one of the Founding Fathers of the United States. His Autobiography, begun in 1771 and left unfinished, describes his plan for practising thirteen virtues.
