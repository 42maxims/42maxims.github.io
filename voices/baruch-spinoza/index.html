Baruch Spinoza

1632–1677RationalismEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Person

Baruch (Benedictus de) Spinoza was a Dutch philosopher of Portuguese-Jewish descent, born in Amsterdam. Expelled from the city's Jewish community in 1656, he earned a modest living grinding lenses and wrote works that shaped modern philosophy, among them the Ethics, published after his death in 1677.
