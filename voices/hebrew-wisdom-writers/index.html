Voice

Hebrew wisdom writers

collected over several centuries; final form c. 5th–4th century BCE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Scripture

The sayings of the Book of Proverbs are traditionally attributed to King Solomon, and the book itself also names other sages, such as Agur and Lemuel. Scholars see it as a collection of Israelite wisdom gathered over several centuries, probably reaching its final form in the Persian or early Hellenistic period.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
