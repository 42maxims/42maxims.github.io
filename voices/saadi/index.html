Saadi

c. 1210–1291/92Persian poetry & literatureMedieval (600 – 1500)Person

Saadi of Shiraz was a Persian poet and prose writer, one of the major figures of classical Persian literature. After years of travel he wrote the Bustan (1257) and the Gulistan (1258), collections of stories, verses and moral sayings that were long used to teach Persian across the Islamic world.
