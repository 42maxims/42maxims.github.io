Upanishadic sages

oldest Upanishads c. 8th–5th century BCEHinduismAncient (before 200 BCE)Scripture

The Upanishads are the late Vedic texts in which teachers and students explore the self, Brahman and right conduct. They were composed and handed down orally by many hands over centuries; the Brihadaranyaka and Taittiriya are among the oldest. Teachers named in them, such as Yajnavalkya, are known only from the texts themselves.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
