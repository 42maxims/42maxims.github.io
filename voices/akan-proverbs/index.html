Akan (Ashanti) proverbs

oral tradition of uncertain age; collected in 1879 and translated in 1916Akan (Ashanti) traditionModern (1800 – 1950)Collective

Proverbs (ebe) carry much of the moral teaching of the Akan peoples of present-day Ghana, including the Asante (Ashanti). The sayings quoted here come from J. G. Christaller's 1879 Twi collection as translated and annotated by the colonial anthropologist R. S. Rattray in 1916.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
