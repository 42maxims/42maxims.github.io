Voice

Ohiyesa (Charles A. Eastman)

1858–1939Dakota (Sioux) traditionModern (1800 – 1950)Person

Charles Alexander Eastman, born Ohiyesa, was a Santee Dakota physician, writer and reformer. Raised in the traditional Dakota way until about fifteen, he later trained in medicine at Boston University and wrote some of the first books by a Native American author about Native life. He had become a Christian, and his accounts of Dakota belief are written from that later vantage and sometimes speak of 'the Indian' in general.
