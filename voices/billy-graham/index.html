Voice

Billy Graham

1918–2018ChristianityContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Billy Graham was an American evangelical preacher whose crusades and books made him one of the best-known Christian figures of the twentieth century. In The Holy Spirit (1978) he retold a parable of two fighting dogs, one fed and one starved, as a picture of the struggle between two natures within a person — a story later popularised, and wrongly credited to Cherokee tradition, as the 'two wolves' tale.
