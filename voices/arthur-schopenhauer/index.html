Arthur Schopenhauer

1788–186019th-century German philosophyModern (1800 – 1950)Person

Arthur Schopenhauer was a German philosopher, born in Danzig, known for the pessimism of his main work, The World as Will and Representation (1818/1819). He was among the first Western philosophers to draw on Indian thought, and he found a wide readership late in life with the essays of Parerga and Paralipomena (1851).
