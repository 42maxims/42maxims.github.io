Voice

Ryan Holiday

born 1987Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Ryan Holiday is an American author and marketer who has done much to bring Stoic philosophy, especially that of Marcus Aurelius, to a modern popular readership. His books include The Obstacle Is the Way (2014) and Ego Is the Enemy (2016).
