Leonardo da Vinci

1452–1519Renaissance art & scienceMedieval (600 – 1500)Person

Leonardo da Vinci was an Italian painter, engineer and scientist of the Renaissance, whose notebooks record his studies of nature, anatomy and machines. The saying 'Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication' is often credited to him, but no source for it in his writings has been found.
