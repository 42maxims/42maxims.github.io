Stephen R. Covey

1932–2012Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Stephen R. Covey was an American educator, author and speaker, and a professor at Utah State University's Jon M. Huntsman School of Business at the time of his death. His best-known book is The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People (1989), and in 1996 Time magazine named him one of the 25 most influential people.
