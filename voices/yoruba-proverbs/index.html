Yoruba proverbs

oral tradition of uncertain age; the sayings here were recorded in 1894Yoruba traditionModern (1800 – 1950)Collective

Proverbs (owe) are a central part of Yoruba speech and teaching in what is now south-western Nigeria, Benin and Togo. The sayings quoted here were collected and translated by the British colonial officer A. B. Ellis, so the English wording is his and the sayings are older than their recording.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
