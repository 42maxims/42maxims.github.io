Voice

James Clear

born 1986Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

James Clear is an American writer, raised in Hamilton, Ohio, who writes about habits and decision-making. He is best known for Atomic Habits (2018), which argues that small, steady changes in behaviour add up to lasting change.
