Marcus Aurelius

121–180 CEStoicismClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Person

Marcus Aurelius was Roman emperor from 161 to 180 CE and a lifelong student of Stoic philosophy. The private notes he wrote to himself, known as the Meditations, are among the most widely read works of Stoicism.
