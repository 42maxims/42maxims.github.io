Voice

Mark Manson

born 1984Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Mark Manson is an American self-help author and blogger. His bestseller The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck (2016) argues that life's struggles give it meaning and pushes back against relentless positive thinking.
