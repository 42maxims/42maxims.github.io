Voice

Andrew Huberman

born 1975Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Andrew Huberman is an American neuroscientist and associate professor of neurobiology and ophthalmology at the Stanford University School of Medicine. Since 2021 he has hosted the widely heard Huberman Lab podcast on health and science; some scientists have criticised claims made on it as poorly evidenced.
