Author of Hebrews

1st century CEChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Known through the text

The Epistle to the Hebrews is an early Christian sermon in letter form that does not name its author. It was long attributed to Paul, but its author is unknown; it was probably written in the second half of the 1st century CE.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
