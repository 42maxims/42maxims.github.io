Plato

c. 428/427 – 348/347 BCEGreek philosophyAncient (before 200 BCE)Person

Plato was an Athenian philosopher, a student of Socrates and the teacher of Aristotle, who founded the Academy in Athens. Most of his works are dialogues in which Socrates is the main speaker, among them the Apology, the Gorgias and the Cratylus.
