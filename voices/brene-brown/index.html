Voice

Brené Brown

born 1965Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Brené Brown is an American academic, author and podcaster who holds an endowed chair at the University of Houston's Graduate College of Social Work. She is known for her research on shame, vulnerability and courage, for her widely viewed 2010 TEDx talk 'The power of vulnerability', and for books such as Daring Greatly (2012).
