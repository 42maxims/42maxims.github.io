Authors of the Avesta

Younger Avestan texts, perhaps 6th–4th century BCE; written down much laterZoroastrianismAncient (before 200 BCE)Scripture

The Avesta is the body of Zoroastrian sacred texts. Apart from the Gathas ascribed to Zarathustra, its hymns, including the Yashts to individual divinities such as Mithra, were composed by generations of priests and handed down orally before being written down in the Sasanian period.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
