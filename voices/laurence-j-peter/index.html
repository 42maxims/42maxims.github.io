Laurence J. Peter

1919–1990Management & engineeringContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Laurence J. Peter was a Canadian-born educator and writer. With Raymond Hull he wrote The Peter Principle (1969), which argues that people in a hierarchy tend to rise to their 'level of incompetence'.
