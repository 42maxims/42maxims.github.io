The Psalmists

composed over several centuries, c. 10th–4th century BCE or later (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Collective

The Book of Psalms gathers 150 songs and prayers of ancient Israel. Many carry headings naming David, and Psalm 90 is headed 'a prayer of Moses'; scholars see the collection as the work of many poets over several centuries, gathered for worship.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
