Blaise Pascal

1623–1662ChristianityEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Person

Blaise Pascal was a French mathematician, physicist and Christian thinker. His Pensées ('Thoughts'), notes towards a defence of Christianity left unfinished at his death, were published in 1670.
