Voice

Joseph M. Juran

1904–2008Management & engineeringModern (1800 – 1950)Person

Joseph M. Juran was a Romanian-born American engineer and management consultant, known for his work on quality management. He popularised the idea of 'the vital few and the trivial many' and named it the Pareto principle, after the economist Vilfredo Pareto.
