Voice

Naval Ravikant

born 1974Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Naval Ravikant is an Indian-born American entrepreneur and investor, co-founder and chairman of AngelList. He hosts his own podcast on philosophy, happiness and wealth, and has been a guest on The Joe Rogan Experience and The Tim Ferriss Show.
