Ptahhotep

set in the 5th Dynasty (c. 25th–24th century BCE); surviving copies date from the Middle Kingdom (c. 20th–19th century BCE)Ancient Egyptian wisdom literatureAncient (before 200 BCE)Traditional or legendary figure

Ptahhotep was a vizier of the Egyptian Old Kingdom to whom The Instruction of Ptahhotep, one of the oldest surviving works of wisdom literature, is credited. Most Egyptologists think the attribution is a literary device and that the text was written down later, in the Middle Kingdom.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
