Voice

Viktor E. Frankl

1905–1997Modern psychology & self-helpModern (1800 – 1950)Person

Viktor Frankl was an Austrian neurologist and psychiatrist who survived the Nazi concentration camps. He founded logotherapy, a school of psychotherapy that sees the search for meaning as the central human motivation, and described his camp experiences in Man's Search for Meaning (1946).
