Author of the Book of Joshua

c. 7th–6th century BCE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Known through the text

The Book of Joshua tells of Israel's entry into Canaan under Joshua, Moses' successor. Jewish tradition attributed it to Joshua himself; most modern scholars see it as part of a longer history composed and edited around the 7th and 6th centuries BCE.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
