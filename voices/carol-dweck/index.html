Voice

Carol S. Dweck

born 1946Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Carol S. Dweck is an American psychologist and professor at Stanford University. Her research on 'fixed' and 'growth' mindsets reached a wide audience through her book Mindset (2006).
