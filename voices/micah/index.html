Voice

Micah

late 8th century BCEJudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Scripture

Micah of Moresheth was a Hebrew prophet in Judah in the late 8th century BCE, a contemporary of Isaiah. The book that bears his name speaks out against injustice and is best known for its summary of what God asks: to do justly, love mercy and walk humbly.
