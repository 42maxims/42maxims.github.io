Voice

Jocko Willink

born 1971Management & engineeringContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Jocko Willink is an American author, podcaster and retired U.S. Navy SEAL officer who served in the Iraq War. He co-wrote Extreme Ownership (2015) with Leif Babin, and after appearing on the podcasts of Tim Ferriss, Joe Rogan and Sam Harris he started the Jocko Podcast in 2015.
