Voice

Jordan B. Peterson

born 1962Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Jordan B. Peterson is a Canadian clinical psychologist and professor emeritus of psychology at the University of Toronto. His self-help book 12 Rules for Life (2018) became an international bestseller.
