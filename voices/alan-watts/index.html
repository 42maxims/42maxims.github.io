Voice

Alan Watts

1915–1973Western interpreters of Zen & TaoismContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Alan Watts was a British-born writer and speaker who spent most of his adult life in the United States. Through books such as The Way of Zen (1957) and many recorded talks, he introduced Zen, Taoism and other Asian philosophies to a wide Western audience.
