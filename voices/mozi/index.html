Mozi

c. 470–391 BCEMohismAncient (before 200 BCE)Person

Mozi (Mo Di) was a Chinese thinker of the Warring States period and founder of the Mohist school. Against the graded loyalties of the Confucians he argued for impartial care (jian ai) for all people, and against offensive war. The book that bears his name was compiled by his followers.
