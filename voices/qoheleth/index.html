Voice

Qoheleth (the Preacher)

c. 450–180 BCE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Scripture

Qoheleth, a Hebrew title usually rendered 'the Preacher' or 'the Teacher', is the voice that speaks in the book of Ecclesiastes. Jewish and Christian tradition identified him with King Solomon; most modern scholars regard the author as unknown and date the book to between the mid-5th and early 2nd centuries BCE.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
