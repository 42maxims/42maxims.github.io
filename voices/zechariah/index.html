Zechariah

active c. 520–518 BCEJudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Person

Zechariah was a Hebrew prophet in Jerusalem after the return from exile in Babylon, who encouraged the rebuilding of the Temple. Scholars generally credit him with chapters 1–8 of the book that bears his name and attribute chapters 9–14 to later writers.
