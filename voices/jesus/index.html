Jesus

c. 6–4 BCE – c. 30/33 CEChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Person

Jesus of Nazareth was a Jewish preacher and teacher in Galilee and Judea under Roman rule. His teaching is preserved in the Gospels of the New Testament, written in the decades after his death; Christians revere him as the Christ and the Son of God.
