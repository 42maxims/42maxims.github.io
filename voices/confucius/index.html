Confucius

551–479 BCE (traditional dates)ConfucianismAncient (before 200 BCE)Person

Kong Qiu, known in the West as Confucius, was a teacher and minor official from the state of Lu in China's Spring and Autumn period. His sayings, gathered by his followers in the Analects, became the heart of the Confucian tradition, with its care for humaneness, ritual propriety and learning.
