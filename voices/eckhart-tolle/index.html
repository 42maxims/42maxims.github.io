Voice

Eckhart Tolle

born 1948Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Eckhart Tolle, born Ulrich Leonard Tölle in Lünen, Germany, is a spiritual teacher and self-help author not tied to any particular religion. His books The Power of Now (1997) and A New Earth (2005) invite readers to rest their attention in the present moment.
