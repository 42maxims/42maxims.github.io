Vyasa

traditional; the Mahabharata grew between about the 4th century BCE and the 4th century CEHinduismClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Traditional or legendary figure

Vyasa is the sage traditionally credited with composing the Mahabharata, in which he also appears as a character. Scholars see the epic as the work of many poets and editors over several centuries, so Vyasa stands for that tradition rather than a single datable author.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
