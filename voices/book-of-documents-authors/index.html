Authors of the Book of Documents

chapters written over many centuries, from the early Zhou to the 4th century CE (scholarly view)ConfucianismAncient (before 200 BCE)Collective

The Book of Documents presents speeches and counsels attributed to the ancient rulers Yao, Shun and Yu and to ministers of the Xia, Shang and Zhou dynasties. Scholars date its chapters very differently: the oldest to the early Zhou, those about the earliest rulers to the Warring States period, and the 'Old Text' chapters to reconstructions of the 3rd or 4th century CE.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
