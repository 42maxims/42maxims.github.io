Hammurabi

reigned c. 1792–1750 BCEAncient Mesopotamia (Babylonian law)Ancient (before 200 BCE)Person

Hammurabi was the sixth Amorite king of Babylon, who brought almost all of Mesopotamia under Babylonian rule. He is best known for the law code issued in his name and inscribed on a basalt stele, now in the Louvre.
