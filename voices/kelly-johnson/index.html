Kelly Johnson

1910–1990Management & engineeringContemporary (1950 – today)Person

Clarence 'Kelly' Johnson was an American aeronautical engineer who led Lockheed's Skunk Works, where aircraft such as the U-2 and SR-71 were designed. The design rule 'keep it simple, stupid' is commonly credited to him, though the attribution rests on later accounts.
