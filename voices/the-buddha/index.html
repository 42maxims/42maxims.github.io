The Buddha

c. 5th century BCE (exact dates debated)BuddhismAncient (before 200 BCE)Person

Siddhartha Gautama, called the Buddha, 'the awakened one', was a wandering teacher in north-eastern India whose teaching became the foundation of Buddhism. His discourses were handed down orally by the monastic community and later written down, most fully in the Pali Canon.
