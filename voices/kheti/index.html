Voice

Kheti (attributed)

set in the First Intermediate Period (c. 2150–2025 BCE); probably composed in the Middle KingdomAncient Egyptian wisdom literatureAncient (before 200 BCE)Traditional or legendary figure

In the Teaching for King Merykara a king of Egypt, possibly named Kheti, advises his son Merykara on how to rule well. The real author is unknown; the text is probably a Middle Kingdom composition set in the earlier period of division.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
