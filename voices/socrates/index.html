Socrates

c. 470–399 BCEGreek philosophyAncient (before 200 BCE)Person

Socrates was an Athenian philosopher who wrote nothing himself and is known mainly through the dialogues of his student Plato and the writings of Xenophon. He was tried and executed in Athens in 399 BCE; how closely Plato's Socrates matches the historical man is still debated.
