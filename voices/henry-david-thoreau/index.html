Voice

Henry David Thoreau

1817–1862TranscendentalismModern (1800 – 1950)Person

Henry David Thoreau was an American essayist, naturalist and a leading voice of New England Transcendentalism. Walden (1854) reflects on the two years he spent living simply in a cabin by Walden Pond, and his essay on civil disobedience has inspired movements of nonviolent resistance.
