Hesiod

active c. 750–650 BCE (scholarly estimate)Ancient Greek epic & dramaAncient (before 200 BCE)Person

Hesiod was an early Greek poet from Ascra in Boeotia, generally thought to have been active around the same time as Homer. His Theogony tells of the origins of the gods, and his Works and Days offers practical and moral advice, addressed to his brother Perses.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
