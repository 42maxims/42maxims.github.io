## 4. Guard your tongue — Speak kindly, or keep silent

**Maxim URL:** https://42maxims.com/maxims/guard-your-tongue/

### Spoken script (~18s)
Speak kindly — or keep silent. Words heal or wound, and you can’t take them back. A hadith puts it plainly: anyone who believes should talk what is good or keep quiet. Proverbs agrees. Check the sources: 42maxims.com.

## 5. Master your anger — Be slow to anger; master yourself

**Maxim URL:** https://42maxims.com/maxims/master-your-anger/

### Spoken script (~20s)
Be slow to anger — master yourself. Real strength isn’t taking a city; it’s ruling your own spirit. That’s Proverbs. The Buddha compares holding back anger to holding a rolling chariot. Same lesson, many sources: 42maxims.com.
