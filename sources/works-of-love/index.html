Works of Love

1847Existentialism / AbsurdismModern (1800 – 1950)Søren KierkegaardBook

A series of Christian reflections that Kierkegaard published under his own name in 1847. They take the command to love one's neighbour as their theme and ask what it means for love to be a duty and a deed.
