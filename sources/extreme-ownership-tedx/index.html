'Extreme Ownership' (TEDxUniversityofNevada talk)

2017Management & engineeringContemporary (1950 – today)Jocko WillinkSpeech or talk

Jocko Willink's TEDx talk at the University of Nevada, published in February 2017. He describes a confused 2006 operation in Ramadi, Iraq, and how taking full responsibility for it, rather than blaming others, earned his commander's trust.
