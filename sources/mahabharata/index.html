Mahabharata

c. 4th century BCE – 4th century CEHinduismClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)VyasaScripture

The great Sanskrit epic of the war between the Pandavas and the Kauravas, and one of the longest poems ever composed. Besides the story it holds long teachings on dharma, including the Bhagavad Gita and the Anusasana Parva on duty and conduct.
