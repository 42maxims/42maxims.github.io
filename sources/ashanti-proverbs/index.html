Source

Ashanti Proverbs

1916Akan (Ashanti) traditionModern (1800 – 1950)Book

R. S. Rattray's English translation, with Twi text and notes, of over 800 proverbs chosen from J. G. Christaller's 1879 Twi collection. Its subtitle reflects colonial attitudes of the time, but it remains a standard early record of Akan proverbs.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
