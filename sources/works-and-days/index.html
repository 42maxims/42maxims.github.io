Works and Days

c. 700 BCEAncient Greek epic & dramaAncient (before 200 BCE)HesiodBook

A didactic poem of 828 lines by Hesiod, written in dactylic hexameter around 700 BCE. At its centre is a farmer's almanac addressed to his brother Perses, set among myths such as Pandora and the ages of humankind and advice on work, justice and good sense.
