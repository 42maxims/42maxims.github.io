Source

Meditations

c. 170–180 CEStoicismClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Marcus AureliusBook

Private notes that Marcus Aurelius wrote in Greek as reflections to himself, some of them while on military campaign. Divided into twelve books, they were probably never meant for publication.
