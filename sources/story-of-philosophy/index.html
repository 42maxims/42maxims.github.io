Source

The Story of Philosophy

1926Popular philosophyModern (1800 – 1950)Will DurantBook

Will Durant's popular survey of Western philosophers from Plato to Dewey, published in 1926. Its chapter on Aristotle contains the line 'We are what we repeatedly do', Durant's own summary, which is often quoted as Aristotle's words.
