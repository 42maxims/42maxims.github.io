Source

Samyutta Nikaya (Connected Discourses)

oral tradition from c. 5th century BCE; written down c. 1st century BCEBuddhismAncient (before 200 BCE)The BuddhaScripture

The third of the five collections of discourses in the Pali Canon, grouping the Buddha's teachings by theme. It includes the Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta, traditionally the Buddha's first sermon, on the Four Noble Truths.
