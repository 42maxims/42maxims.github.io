12 Rules for Life: An Antidote to Chaos

2018Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Jordan B. PetersonBook

A self-help book by Jordan B. Peterson, published in 2018, offering twelve practical rules for living that draw on psychology, mythology, religion and personal stories. It became an international bestseller.
