Lives of Eminent Philosophers

probably first half of the 3rd century CEGreek philosophyClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Diogenes LaertiusBook

Ten books of biographies and sayings of Greek philosophers, from the earliest sages to Epicurus. Book VII covers Zeno of Citium and the Stoics; for many thinkers it is our main surviving source.
