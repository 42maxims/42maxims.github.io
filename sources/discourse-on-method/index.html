Discourse on the Method

1637RationalismEarly modern (1500 – 1800)René DescartesBook

René Descartes's short work, published in French in 1637, describing how he sought certain knowledge by doubting everything that could be doubted. Part IV contains the famous 'I think, therefore I am'.
