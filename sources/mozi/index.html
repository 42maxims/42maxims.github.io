Mozi

c. 5th–3rd century BCEMohismAncient (before 200 BCE)MoziBook

The collected writings of the Mohist school, named after its founder. Its core chapters argue for impartial care for all, against aggressive war and against waste, often in three versions that may come from different branches of the school.
