Exodus

final form c. 5th century BCE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Authors of the TorahScripture

The second book of the Torah, telling of Israel's liberation from slavery in Egypt under Moses and the covenant made at Mount Sinai. It contains the Ten Commandments.
