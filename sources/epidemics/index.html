Epidemics

c. late 5th – 4th century BCEHippocratic medicineAncient (before 200 BCE)HippocratesBook

Seven books of case notes and observations on illness in the Hippocratic Corpus. They are traditionally attributed to Hippocrates, though their authorship is uncertain; Book I contains the advice to the physician to help, or at least to do no harm.
