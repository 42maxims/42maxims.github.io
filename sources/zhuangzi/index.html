Source

Zhuangzi

c. 4th–2nd century BCETaoismAncient (before 200 BCE)ZhuangziBook

The second great classic of Taoism, in 33 chapters of stories, dialogues and reflections on freedom, change and the limits of knowledge. Only the Inner Chapters are usually credited to Zhuangzi himself.
