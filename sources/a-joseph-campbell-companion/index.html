Source

A Joseph Campbell Companion: Reflections on the Art of Living

1991Comparative mythologyContemporary (1950 – today)Joseph CampbellCollection

A posthumous collection of short sayings, published in 1991 from material gathered by Diane K. Osbon, who said she wrote them down in her journals over years in Campbell's company. Because the lines were recorded by a listener rather than written by Campbell, their exact wording rests on her notes.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
