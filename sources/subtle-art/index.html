The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck

2016Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Mark MansonBook

Mark Manson's 2016 self-help bestseller. It argues that struggle gives life its meaning and that chasing constant positivity only makes us feel worse.
