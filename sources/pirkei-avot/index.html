Pirkei Avot (Ethics of the Fathers)

compiled c. 200 CEJudaismClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Collection

A tractate of the Mishnah gathering the ethical sayings of the early rabbis, from the Men of the Great Assembly to the generations after the Second Temple, with a sixth chapter added later. The Mishnah was compiled under Rabbi Judah ha-Nasi around 200 CE.
