Gospel of John

c. 90–100 CE (scholarly estimate)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Scripture

The fourth gospel of the New Testament, telling of the life, teaching, death and resurrection of Jesus. It is traditionally attributed to the apostle John; the text itself names an unnamed 'disciple whom Jesus loved' as its source, and most scholars date its completion to around 90–100 CE.
