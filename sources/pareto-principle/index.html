Pareto principle (the 80/20 rule)

Pareto's work on distribution 1896–1906; developed and named by Juran from 1941Management & engineeringModern (1800 – 1950)Joseph M. JuranOther

The observation that, for many outcomes, roughly 80% of effects come from 20% of causes. It is named after the Italian economist Vilfredo Pareto, who noted that about 80% of the land in Italy was owned by 20% of the people; Joseph Juran generalised the idea and gave it its name.
