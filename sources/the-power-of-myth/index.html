The Power of Myth

1988Comparative mythologyContemporary (1950 – today)Joseph CampbellBook

The companion book to Joseph Campbell and the Power of Myth, six conversations with the journalist Bill Moyers filmed in the last two summers of Campbell's life and broadcast on PBS in 1988. In them Campbell talks about the hero's journey, the place of myth in modern life and his advice to 'follow your bliss'.
