Pensées

written 1650s–1662; published 1670ChristianityEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Blaise PascalBook

Fragments that Blaise Pascal wrote towards a defence of the Christian faith, left unfinished at his death in 1662 and published in 1670. They reflect on human restlessness, the limits of reason and the search for God.
