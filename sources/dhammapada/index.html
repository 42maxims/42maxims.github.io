Source

Dhammapada

compiled c. 3rd century BCE (estimates vary)BuddhismAncient (before 200 BCE)The BuddhaScripture

A collection of 423 verses of the Buddha's teaching in Pali, part of the Pali Canon. It is one of the most widely read and translated Buddhist texts.
