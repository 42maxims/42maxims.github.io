Source

Ecclesiastes

c. 5th–3rd century BCE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Qoheleth (the Preacher)Scripture

A book of the Hebrew Bible's wisdom literature in which Qoheleth, 'the Preacher', reflects on the brevity of life and the vanity of striving. It is traditionally attributed to Solomon; most scholars date it to the Persian or Hellenistic period.
