First Epistle to the Corinthians

c. 53–57 CEChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Paul the ApostleScripture

Paul's letter to the Christian congregation at Corinth, in Greece, written to address problems that had arisen there, probably from Ephesus in the mid-50s CE. It includes the well-known passage on love in chapter 13. Scholars agree that Paul wrote it.
