Source

Book of Psalms

composed c. 10th–4th century BCE or later; collection fixed c. 1st century CE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)The PsalmistsScripture

A collection of 150 songs and prayers of praise, lament and thanksgiving (in the Jewish and Western Christian numbering; Eastern churches include a few more), used in Jewish and Christian worship to this day. Many psalms are traditionally attributed to King David.
