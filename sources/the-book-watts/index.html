The Book: On the Taboo Against Knowing Who You Are

1966Western interpreters of Zen & TaoismContemporary (1950 – today)Alan WattsBook

A short book by Alan Watts, published in 1966, which draws on Vedanta and Zen to question the sense of being a separate self. The quotation shown here has been checked only through Wikiquote, not against the book itself.
