The Book: On the Taboo Against Knowing Who You Are

1966Western interpreters of Zen & TaoismContemporary (1950 – today)Alan WattsBook

A short book by Alan Watts, published in 1966, which draws on Vedanta and Zen to question the sense of being a separate self. The quotation shown here was checked in the Internet Archive scan of the 1966 Pantheon edition.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
