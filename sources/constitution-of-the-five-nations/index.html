The Constitution of the Five Nations

1916Haudenosaunee (Iroquois) traditionModern (1800 – 1950)The Great Law of Peace (Haudenosaunee)Book

Arthur C. Parker's edition of the Great Law of the Haudenosaunee (Iroquois) Confederacy, published by the New York State Museum in 1916. It is based chiefly on a manuscript compiled by the Mohawk Seth Newhouse and revised by Albert Cusick, together with a version prepared by a committee of Six Nations chiefs.
