Book of Zechariah

chapters 1–8 c. 520–518 BCE; chapters 9–14 laterJudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)ZechariahScripture

One of the twelve Minor Prophets of the Hebrew Bible. Its first eight chapters record the visions of the prophet Zechariah as the Temple was being rebuilt after the exile.
