Source

Avesta

composed c. 1500–400 BCE; written down c. 5th–6th century CEZoroastrianismAncient (before 200 BCE)Authors of the AvestaScripture

The sacred texts of Zoroastrianism, in the Avestan language. Besides the Gathas and the liturgy it includes the Yashts, hymns to individual divinities; the Mihr Yasht praises Mithra as guardian of contracts and of the truth.
