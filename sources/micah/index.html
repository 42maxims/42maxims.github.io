Book of Micah

core late 8th century BCE; edited laterJudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)MicahScripture

One of the twelve Minor Prophets of the Hebrew Bible, named for Micah of Moresheth. It condemns injustice and contains the well-known summary 'to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God'.
