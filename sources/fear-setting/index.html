'Fear-Setting' (blog post)

2017Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Tim FerrissOther

A 2017 post on Tim Ferriss's blog, adapted from chapters of The 4-Hour Workweek (2007) and Tools of Titans (2016). It sets out his 'fear-setting' exercise of naming the worst case, accepting it and acting anyway.
