Atomic Habits

2018Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)James ClearBook

James Clear's 2018 book on building good habits and breaking bad ones. Its central idea is that small, easy changes, repeated daily, add up to large ones and gradually reshape how we see ourselves.
