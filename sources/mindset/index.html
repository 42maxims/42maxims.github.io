Mindset: The New Psychology of Success

2006Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Carol S. DweckBook

Carol S. Dweck's book, published in 2006, on the difference between a 'fixed' mindset, which sees ability as set, and a 'growth' mindset, which sees it as something that can be developed.
