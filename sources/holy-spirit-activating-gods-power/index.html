The Holy Spirit: Activating God's Power in Your Life

1978ChristianityContemporary (1950 – today)Billy GrahamBook

A 1978 book by Billy Graham on the work of the Holy Spirit in Christian life. Chapter 7, 'The Christian's Inner Struggle', includes his parable of an Eskimo fisherman's two dogs — the ancestor of the popular 'two wolves' internet story, which is often misattributed to Cherokee tradition.
