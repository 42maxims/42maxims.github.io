Source

The Instruction of Ptahhotep

Middle Kingdom copies, c. 20th–19th century BCEAncient Egyptian wisdom literatureAncient (before 200 BCE)PtahhotepBook

An Egyptian wisdom text in which an aged vizier gives his son advice on conduct, speech, humility and justice. It survives mainly in the Prisse Papyrus and is among the oldest books of moral instruction in the world.
