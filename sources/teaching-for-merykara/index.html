Teaching for King Merykara

probably Middle Kingdom, c. 2025–1700 BCEAncient Egyptian wisdom literatureAncient (before 200 BCE)Kheti (attributed)Book

A Middle Egyptian instruction in which a king of the First Intermediate Period advises his son Merykara on how to be a good king and avoid evil, including how to treat the weak and judge justly.
