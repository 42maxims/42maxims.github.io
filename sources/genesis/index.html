Source

Genesis

final form c. 5th century BCE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Authors of the TorahScripture

The first book of the Torah, telling of the creation of the world, the first humans in the garden of Eden, and the stories of Noah, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob and Joseph. In its second creation account the first human is placed in the garden to tend and keep it.
