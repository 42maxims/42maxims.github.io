Source

Taittiriya Upanishad

c. 6th–5th century BCEHinduismAncient (before 200 BCE)Upanishadic sagesScripture

One of the principal Upanishads, attached to the Black Yajurveda. Its first part ends with a teacher's famous charge to the graduating student: speak the truth, do your duty, and honour mother, father, teacher and guest.
