Source

Nicomachean Ethics

c. 4th century BCEGreek philosophyAncient (before 200 BCE)AristotleBook

Aristotle's main work on ethics, probably based on his lectures at the Lyceum. It asks what makes a human life go well and describes virtue as a habit of choosing the mean between excess and deficiency.
