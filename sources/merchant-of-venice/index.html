Source

The Merchant of Venice

c. 1596–1599English poetry & literatureEarly modern (1500 – 1800)William ShakespeareBook

A comedy by William Shakespeare, written in the late 1590s and first printed in 1600. In the trial scene (Act 4, Scene 1) Portia, disguised as a lawyer, pleads for mercy in a speech that became one of the best-known passages in English.
