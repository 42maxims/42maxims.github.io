The Obstacle Is the Way

2014Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Ryan HolidayBook

Ryan Holiday's 2014 book on turning obstacles into opportunities. Its title and theme come from a line in Marcus Aurelius's Meditations (V.20) about what stands in the way becoming the way.
