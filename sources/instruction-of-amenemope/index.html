Instruction of Amenemope

probably Ramesside period, c. 1300–1075 BCEAncient Egyptian wisdom literatureAncient (before 200 BCE)AmenemopeBook

An Egyptian wisdom text of thirty chapters of advice, ostensibly from the scribe Amenemope to his son. It reflects the New Kingdom "Age of Personal Piety" and is known for its similarity to part of the Book of Proverbs.
