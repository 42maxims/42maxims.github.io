Acaranga Sutra

c. 5th–3rd century BCE (first book c. 5th–4th century BCE; second book later)JainismAncient (before 200 BCE)MahaviraScripture

The first of the twelve Angas, the core scriptures of Svetambara Jainism (eleven of which survive), and generally considered the oldest. It sets out the conduct of Jain monks and nuns, with a strong emphasis on non-violence towards all living beings.
