Source

Brihadaranyaka Upanishad

c. 7th–6th century BCEHinduismAncient (before 200 BCE)Upanishadic sagesScripture

One of the oldest and longest Upanishads, attached to the White Yajurveda. It contains the dialogues of the sage Yajnavalkya on the self and the parable of the thunder's 'Da Da Da': self-restraint, giving and compassion.
