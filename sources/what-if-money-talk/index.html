'What if money was no object?' (lecture excerpt)

unknownWestern interpreters of Zen & TaoismContemporary (1950 – today)Alan WattsTalk, video or podcast

A short recorded excerpt from one of Alan Watts's lectures, widely shared online under this title. We have not traced the original lecture or its date, and the wording quoted here is a popular paraphrase rather than a transcript.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.

The second clause is widely quoted from the talk in books (seen via Internet Archive full-text search) but no transcript or recording was checked; "Do what you love, the money will follow" is not his phrase (it is the title of a 1987 book by Marsha Sinetar)
