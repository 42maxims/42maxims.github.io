Moral Letters to Lucilius (Epistulae Morales ad Lucilium)

c. 62–65 CEStoicismClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)SenecaBook

A collection of 124 surviving letters that Seneca wrote near the end of his life, after retiring from Nero's service, addressed to his friend Lucilius, then procurator of Sicily. Written as practical advice on living well, they cover fear, grief, friendship, time and death.
