Source

Book of Documents (Shujing)

compiled over many centuries; the received text includes 'Old Text' chapters judged 3rd–4th-century CE reconstructionsConfucianismAncient (before 200 BCE)Authors of the Book of DocumentsBook

One of the Five Classics of ancient Chinese literature, a collection of speeches and documents attributed to early rulers and ministers, and a foundation of Chinese political thought for over two millennia. In the 17th century the scholar Yan Ruoqu persuaded most scholars that the 'Old Text' chapters of the received text were fabricated in the 3rd or 4th century CE.

Authorship, dates or origin are uncertain or disputed; read this background with care.
