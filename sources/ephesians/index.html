Epistle to the Ephesians

c. 62 CE (traditional) or c. 70–100 CE (many scholars)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Paul the ApostleScripture

A letter on the unity of the church, written in Paul's name and traditionally addressed to Christians at Ephesus; some scholars see it as a circular letter. It is traditionally attributed to Paul; many modern scholars think it was written by a later follower.
