The Peter Principle

1969Management & engineeringContemporary (1950 – today)Laurence J. PeterBook

A humorous book by Laurence J. Peter and Raymond Hull, published in 1969, on how organisations work. Its central claim is that in a hierarchy every employee tends to rise to their level of incompetence.
