Source

Jami' at-Tirmidhi

compiled 9th century CEIslamMedieval (600 – 1500)The Prophet MuhammadCollection

A collection of hadith compiled by Muhammad ibn Isa al-Tirmidhi (824–892 CE), one of the six major hadith collections of Sunni Islam. Al-Tirmidhi notes his own assessment of the reliability of many reports.
