Source

Leviticus

final form c. 5th century BCE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Authors of the TorahScripture

The third book of the Torah, setting out laws of worship, purity and holiness for ancient Israel. Its Holiness Code includes the command to love your neighbour as yourself, and to love the stranger.
