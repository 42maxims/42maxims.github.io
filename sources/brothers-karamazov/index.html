The Brothers Karamazov

1879–1880Russian literature & thoughtModern (1800 – 1950)Fyodor DostoevskyBook

Dostoevsky's last novel, first published in instalments in 1879–80. Through three brothers, their father's murder and the elder Zossima, it weighs faith and doubt, freedom and responsibility.

    — Fyodor Dostoevsky, The Brothers Karamazov (1879–80), Book II, ch. 2 (Father Zossima), tr. Constance Garnett
