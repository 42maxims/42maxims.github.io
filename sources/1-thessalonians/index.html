First Epistle to the Thessalonians

c. 50 CEChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Paul the ApostleScripture

Paul's letter to the young church at Thessalonica in Macedonia. It is generally considered the earliest of his surviving letters and possibly the oldest book of the New Testament.
