Source

The Analects (Lunyu)

compiled c. 5th–3rd century BCE; final form c. 2nd century BCE (Han dynasty)ConfucianismAncient (before 200 BCE)ConfuciusBook

A collection of short sayings and conversations of Confucius and his disciples, put together by later generations of his followers. It is the most direct record of his teaching and one of the Four Books of the Confucian canon.
