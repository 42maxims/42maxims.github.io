The Autobiography of Benjamin Franklin

written 1771–1790; first published 1791Enlightenment thoughtEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Benjamin FranklinBook

Benjamin Franklin's unfinished account of his life, written in several stages from 1771 and first published, in French, in 1791. It includes his plan of thirteen virtues, practised one week at a time.
