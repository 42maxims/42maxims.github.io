Source

Anguttara Nikaya (Numerical Discourses)

oral tradition from c. 5th century BCE; written down c. 1st century BCEBuddhismAncient (before 200 BCE)The BuddhaScripture

The fourth of the five collections of discourses in the Pali Canon. Its several thousand discourses, ascribed to the Buddha and his chief disciples, are arranged in eleven books by the number of items each teaching lists.

  • “Abandoning divisive speech he abstains from divisive speech. What he has heard here he does not tell there to break those people apart from these people here. What he has heard there he does not tell here to break these people apart from those people there.”
    — The Buddha, Cunda Kammaraputta Sutta (AN 10.176), tr. Thanissaro Bhikkhu; divisive speech is listed apart from abusive speech
