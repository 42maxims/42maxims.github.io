Source

Tao Te Ching (Daodejing)

c. 4th century BCE (estimates range from the 6th to the 3rd century BCE)TaoismAncient (before 200 BCE)LaoziScripture

A short classic of 81 chapters on the Tao, the Way, and its power or virtue, traditionally attributed to Laozi. It is the founding text of Taoism and one of the most translated books in the world.
