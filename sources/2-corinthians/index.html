Second Epistle to the Corinthians

mid-50s CE (scholarly estimate)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Paul the ApostleScripture

A further letter from Paul, with Timothy named as co-sender, to the Christians at Corinth, usually dated after Paul left Ephesus for Macedonia. Scholars have little doubt that Paul wrote it, though they discuss whether it was first a single letter or was put together from two or more of his letters.
