The Gulag Archipelago

written 1958–1968; published 1973Russian literature & thoughtContemporary (1950 – today)Aleksandr SolzhenitsynBook

Solzhenitsyn's three-volume history of the Soviet forced-labour camp system, built from his own imprisonment and the testimony of many former prisoners. It was first published in Paris in 1973 and was not openly published in the Soviet Union until 1989.
