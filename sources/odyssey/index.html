Odyssey

c. 8th century BCE (estimates vary)Ancient Greek epic & dramaAncient (before 200 BCE)HomerBook

An epic poem in 24 books attributed to Homer, telling of Odysseus's ten-year journey home after the Trojan War. In Book XIV the swineherd Eumaeus welcomes him, disguised as a beggar, as an honoured guest.
