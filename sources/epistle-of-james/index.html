Epistle of James

mid-1st to mid-2nd century CE (date disputed)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)JamesScripture

A short New Testament letter of practical wisdom, urging its readers to put faith into action. It is traditionally attributed to James the brother of Jesus; its authorship and date are debated.
