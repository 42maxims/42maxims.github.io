First Epistle to Timothy

c. 60s CE (traditional) or late 1st–early 2nd century CE (many scholars)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Paul the ApostleScripture

One of the three 'Pastoral Epistles', giving guidance to Paul's co-worker Timothy on leading a church. It is traditionally attributed to Paul; most critical scholars think it was written in his name some decades after his death.
