An Essay on Criticism

1711English poetry & literatureEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Alexander PopeBook

A poem in rhyming couplets by Alexander Pope, published in 1711, on the art of writing and judging literature. Many of its lines became proverbs, including 'a little learning is a dangerous thing'.
