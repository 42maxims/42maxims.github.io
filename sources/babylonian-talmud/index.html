Babylonian Talmud

compiled c. 3rd–6th century CEJudaismClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Rabbis of the TalmudScripture

The central text of rabbinic Judaism after the Hebrew Bible: the Mishnah together with the Gemara, the discussions of generations of rabbis in Babylonia. It was shaped over several centuries and reached its final form around the 6th century CE.
