Source

Sunan Ibn Majah

compiled 9th century CEIslamMedieval (600 – 1500)The Prophet MuhammadScripture

A collection of hadith compiled by Ibn Majah (824–887 CE), counted as the sixth of the six major hadith collections of Sunni Islam. It contains some reports graded weak, so each narration is assessed individually.
