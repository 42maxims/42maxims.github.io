Last message to Scouts

written before 1941; published after his death in 1941Scouting movementModern (1800 – 1950)Robert Baden-PowellOther

A farewell letter that Robert Baden-Powell prepared for the Scouts of the world, to be published after his death; it appeared after he died in Kenya in January 1941. It contains his advice to try to leave the world a little better than you found it.
