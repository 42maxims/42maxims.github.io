Source

The Hidden Words

c. 1858Bahá’í FaithModern (1800 – 1950)Bahá’u’lláhScripture

A short collection of 71 Arabic and 82 Persian aphorisms by Bahá’u’lláh, written in Baghdad, on the spiritual and moral life. The standard English translation is by Shoghi Effendi.
