Source

The Qur'an

revealed c. 610–632 CE; standard text compiled c. 650 CEIslamMedieval (600 – 1500)The Qur'anScripture

The central scripture of Islam, in 114 suras (chapters), believed by Muslims to be the word of God revealed to the Prophet Muhammad. Translations used here are by J. M. Rodwell and Marmaduke Pickthall.
