Live Not by Lies

1974Russian literature & thoughtContemporary (1950 – today)Aleksandr SolzhenitsynOther

A short essay Solzhenitsyn released on 12 February 1974, the day of his arrest, just before he was exiled from the Soviet Union. It asks ordinary people not to overthrow anything, only to refuse, each personally, to take part in lies.
