Walden; or, Life in the Woods

1854TranscendentalismModern (1800 – 1950)Henry David ThoreauBook

Henry David Thoreau's account, published in 1854, of the two years he spent living in a cabin near Walden Pond in Massachusetts. It is a reflection on simple living, self-reliance and attention to nature.
