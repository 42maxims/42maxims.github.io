Source

Sutta Nipata

oral tradition from c. 5th century BCE; written down c. 1st century BCEBuddhismAncient (before 200 BCE)The BuddhaScripture

A collection of discourses in verse and prose in the Pali Canon, widely regarded as containing some of its oldest material. It includes the Metta Sutta on loving-kindness and the Mangala Sutta on the highest blessings.
