The Myth of Sisyphus

1942Existentialism / AbsurdismModern (1800 – 1950)Albert CamusBook

A philosophical essay by Albert Camus, first published in French in 1942, which asks whether life is worth living if it has no ultimate meaning. It ends with Sisyphus pushing his rock up the hill forever, and Camus's conclusion that we must imagine him happy.
