Groundwork of the Metaphysic of Morals

1785Enlightenment thoughtEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Immanuel KantBook

A short work in which Kant seeks the supreme principle of morality and states it as the categorical imperative, including the formulas of universal law and of humanity as an end in itself. The public-domain English translation by T. K. Abbott is titled Fundamental Principles of the Metaphysic of Morals.
