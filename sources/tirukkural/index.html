Source

Tirukkural

c. 1st century BCE – 5th century CE (estimates vary)Tamil ethics (Tirukkural)Classical (200 BCE – 600 CE)TiruvalluvarBook

A classic Tamil work of 1,330 two-line couplets arranged in 133 chapters on virtue, wealth and love. It is valued for its broad, largely non-sectarian ethics and is one of the most translated works in Indian literature.
