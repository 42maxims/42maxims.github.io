Source

Gospel of Luke

c. 80–90 CE (most common scholarly estimate)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Scripture

The third Gospel of the New Testament, which tells the story of Jesus and includes parables found nowhere else, such as the Good Samaritan and the Prodigal Son. It is traditionally attributed to Luke, a companion of Paul, and forms a two-part work with the Acts of the Apostles.
