Epistle to the Colossians

c. 60–80 CE (disputed)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Paul the ApostleScripture

A letter to the church at Colossae in Asia Minor, written in Paul's name. It is traditionally attributed to Paul; many scholars think a follower wrote it after his death, while others defend Paul's authorship.
