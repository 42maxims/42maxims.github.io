Epistle to the Romans

c. 57 CEChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Paul the ApostleScripture

Paul's letter to the Christians in Rome, written around 57 CE, is the longest and most systematic of his letters. Scholars agree that Paul wrote it.
