The Power of Now

1997Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Eckhart TolleBook

Eckhart Tolle's 1997 guide to living in the present moment, with simple exercises in self-observation. Oprah Winfrey recommended it, and it has been translated into 33 languages.
