The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People

1989Modern psychology & self-helpContemporary (1950 – today)Stephen R. CoveyBook

Stephen R. Covey's self-help and business book, first published in 1989. It sets out seven habits for personal and working effectiveness and puts character ahead of technique.
