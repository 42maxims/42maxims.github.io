Source

Gorgias

c. 380s BCEGreek philosophyAncient (before 200 BCE)PlatoBook

A dialogue by Plato in which Socrates questions the orator Gorgias and his followers about rhetoric, power and justice. In it Socrates argues that it is better to suffer wrong than to do it.
