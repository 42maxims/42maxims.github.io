Epistle to the Galatians

late 40s–early 50s CE (majority scholarly view)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Paul the ApostleScripture

A letter of the New Testament from Paul the Apostle to early Christian communities in Galatia, in Anatolia. It argues that non-Jewish followers of Jesus need not keep the Mosaic Law, especially circumcision, and closes with counsel on living by the Spirit. Scholars agree it is Paul's own writing.
