Source

Book of Joshua

c. 7th–6th century BCE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Author of the Book of JoshuaScripture

The sixth book of the Hebrew Bible, telling of Israel's entry into Canaan under Joshua. Tradition attributes it to Joshua; modern scholars see it as part of a history edited over the 7th and 6th centuries BCE.
