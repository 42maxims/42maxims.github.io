Essays

1580; expanded 1588Renaissance humanismEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Michel de MontaigneBook

Montaigne's Essais, a collection of 107 essays in three books, first published in 1580 and expanded in 1588. Drawing on classical authors and his own experience, they range from friendship and solitude to death and the limits of knowledge.
