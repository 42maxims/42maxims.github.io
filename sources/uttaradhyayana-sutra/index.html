Source

Uttaradhyayana Sutra

c. 3rd–1st century BCE (oldest parts); the Svetambara canon was fixed in the 5th century CEJainismAncient (before 200 BCE)MahaviraScripture

A Svetambara Jain scripture of 36 lectures, traditionally held to be Mahavira's last sermon. It teaches the monastic life through verses, parables and dialogues, with frequent reminders of how short life is.
