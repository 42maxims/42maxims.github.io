Source

Cratylus

c. 4th century BCEGreek philosophyAncient (before 200 BCE)PlatoBook

A dialogue by Plato on whether names belong to things by nature or by convention. In it Socrates reports Heraclitus's saying that you cannot step into the same river twice.
