Source

Sahih Muslim

compiled 9th century CEIslamMedieval (600 – 1500)The Prophet MuhammadScripture

A collection of hadith compiled by Muslim ibn al-Hajjaj (c. 815–875 CE; his birth year is uncertain). Sunni Muslims regard it, with Sahih al-Bukhari, as one of the two most authentic collections.
