Source

Bhagavad Gita

c. 2nd–1st century BCE (estimates range from the 5th century BCE to the 2nd century CE)HinduismAncient (before 200 BCE)KrishnaScripture

A 700-verse Sanskrit dialogue between the prince Arjuna and Krishna, his charioteer, set within the epic Mahabharata. Traditionally attributed to the sage Vyasa, it is one of the most revered texts of Hinduism.
