Structured Programming with go to Statements

1974Computer scienceContemporary (1950 – today)Donald KnuthArticle or paper

A paper by Donald Knuth published in ACM Computing Surveys in December 1974, on program structure and efficiency. It is the source of his often-quoted remark that premature optimization is the root of all evil, together with his advice not to neglect the critical few per cent of a program.

    — Donald Knuth, "Structured Programming with go to Statements", Computing Surveys 6:4 (1974), §1 (checked via Wikiquote and Wikipedia, not the original)
