Source

Gospel of Matthew

c. 80–90 CE (scholarly estimate)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Scripture

The first book of the New Testament, telling of the life, teaching, death and resurrection of Jesus, including the Sermon on the Mount. It is traditionally attributed to the apostle Matthew; most scholars think it was written anonymously around 80–90 CE, drawing on the Gospel of Mark.
