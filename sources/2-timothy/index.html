Second Epistle to Timothy

c. 60s CE (traditional) or c. 90–140 CE (many scholars)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Paul the ApostleScripture

One of the 'Pastoral Epistles', presented as Paul's last letter to Timothy, written from prison. It is traditionally attributed to Paul; many scholars think it was written in his name after his death.
