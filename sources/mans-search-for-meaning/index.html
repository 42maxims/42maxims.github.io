Man's Search for Meaning

1946Modern psychology & self-helpModern (1800 – 1950)Viktor E. FranklBook

Viktor Frankl's 1946 account of his years as a prisoner in Nazi concentration camps, followed by an outline of logotherapy. It describes finding meaning through work, through love for another person, and through facing unavoidable suffering with dignity.
