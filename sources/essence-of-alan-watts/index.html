A collection based on Alan Watts's 1971 television series, published in 1977, a few years after his death, and later reissued as 'You Are the Universe in Motion'. The quotation shown here has been checked only through Wikiquote, not against the book itself.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
