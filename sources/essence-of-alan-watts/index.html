The Essence of Alan Watts

1977Western interpreters of Zen & TaoismContemporary (1950 – today)Alan WattsBook

A collection based on Alan Watts's 1971 television series, published in 1977, a few years after his death, and later reissued as 'You Are the Universe in Motion'. The quotation shown here has been checked only through Wikiquote, not against the book itself.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.

Quotes (1)

  • “This is the real secret of life — to be completely engaged with what you are doing in the here and now. And instead of calling it work, realize it is play.”
    — Alan Watts, The Essential Alan Watts (Celestial Arts, Berkeley), reissue of The Essence of Alan Watts (1977); checked in the Internet Archive scan of that edition. The 1977 Millbrae edition reads "realize that it is play"
