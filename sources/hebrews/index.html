Epistle to the Hebrews

c. 60–95 CE (scholarly estimate)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Author of HebrewsScripture

An early Christian sermon in letter form, encouraging its readers to hold fast to their faith. Its author is unknown; it was long attributed to Paul, which most scholars now doubt.
