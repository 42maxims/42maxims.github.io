Gulistan (The Rose Garden)

1258Persian poetry & literatureMedieval (600 – 1500)SaadiBook

Saadi's collection of short stories, verses and maxims in eight chapters, on kings, dervishes, contentment, silence, youth, old age, education and conduct. For centuries it was a standard school text in the Persian-speaking world.
