Epistle to the Philippians

c. 50s–early 60s CEChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Paul the ApostleScripture

A warm letter from Paul to the church he founded at Philippi in Macedonia, written while he was in prison. It is one of the letters scholars agree Paul wrote himself.
