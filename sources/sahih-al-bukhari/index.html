Source

Sahih al-Bukhari

compiled 9th century CEIslamMedieval (600 – 1500)The Prophet MuhammadCollection

A collection of hadith, reports of the Prophet Muhammad's words and deeds, compiled by Muhammad al-Bukhari (810–870 CE). Sunni Muslims regard it as the most authentic of the hadith collections.
