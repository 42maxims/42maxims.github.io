Source

Book of Proverbs

collected over centuries; final form c. 5th–4th century BCE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Hebrew wisdom writersScripture

A collection of short sayings on wisdom, work, speech and right living, in the wisdom literature of the Hebrew Bible. It is traditionally attributed to King Solomon, though it gathers material from several sources and periods.
