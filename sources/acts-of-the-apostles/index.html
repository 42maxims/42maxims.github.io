Acts of the Apostles

c. 80–90 CE (most common scholarly estimate)ChristianityClassical (200 BCE – 600 CE)Scripture

The continuation of Luke's Gospel, telling of the first Christian communities and the journeys of Paul. Like the Gospel, it is traditionally attributed to Luke.
