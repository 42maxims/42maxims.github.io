Source

The Yoruba-Speaking Peoples of the Slave Coast of West Africa

1894Yoruba traditionModern (1800 – 1950)Book

An ethnography by A. B. Ellis, a British army officer in West Africa. Its chapter on proverbs gives several hundred Yoruba sayings in English translation; the framing reflects colonial attitudes of its time, but the proverbs are recorded with their Yoruba originals.

Little is known for certain here; this background is thin and may be revised.
