Source

Deuteronomy

core c. 7th century BCE; final form c. 5th century BCE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Authors of the TorahScripture

The fifth book of the Torah, presented as Moses' farewell speeches to Israel before it enters the promised land. Many scholars link its core to religious reforms in Judah in the 7th century BCE.
