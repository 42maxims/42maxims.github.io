Treatise of the Most Exalted One on Action and Response (Taishang Ganying Pian)

12th centuryTaoismMedieval (600 – 1500)Treatise on Action and ResponseBook

A short Daoist tract listing good and bad deeds and the rewards and punishments they bring. Widely printed and given away as an act of merit, it was translated by James Legge as 'The Thai-Shang Tractate of Actions and Their Retributions'.
