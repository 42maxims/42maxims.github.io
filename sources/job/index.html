Book of Job

c. 6th–4th century BCE (scholarly estimate)JudaismAncient (before 200 BCE)Author of the Book of JobScripture

A poetic book of the Hebrew Bible in which a righteous man who loses everything wrestles with God and his friends over why the innocent suffer. Its author is unknown.
