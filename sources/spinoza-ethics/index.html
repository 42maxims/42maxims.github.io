Source

Ethics

completed c. 1675; published 1677RationalismEarly modern (1500 – 1800)Baruch SpinozaBook

Spinoza's main work, set out in the manner of geometry with definitions, propositions and proofs. It moves from God and nature to the human emotions and argues that freedom and blessedness come through understanding. It was published shortly after his death in 1677.
