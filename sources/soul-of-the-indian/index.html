Source

The Soul of the Indian

1911Dakota (Sioux) traditionModern (1800 – 1950)Ohiyesa (Charles A. Eastman)Book

Charles Eastman's short account of the religion and moral life of the Dakota as he knew it before contact with white culture. He writes as both an insider and a convert to Christianity, and often generalises about Native peoples as a whole.
