Themes

The maxims grouped by what they are about, for readers who arrive with a question rather than a quote. A maxim can sit in more than one theme.

    5 maxims

    Forgive, and keep forgiving., Be slow to anger; master yourself., Meet hatred with love and evil with good., Seek peace, and pursue it., Be patient; endure, and let things ripen.

  • Speech: honesty, gossip and listening
    6 maxims

    Tell the truth, or at least don't lie., Speak kindly, or keep silent., Don't speak ill of people behind their backs., Let your actions match your words., Keep your promises; let your yes mean yes., Listen more than you speak.

  • Kindness and compassion
    6 maxims

    Treat others as you would wish to be treated., Be merciful to every living being., Give freely; generosity enriches the giver., Welcome the stranger., First, do no harm., Be kind to animals.

  • Justice and fairness
    5 maxims

    Treat others as you would wish to be treated., Defend the widow, the orphan and the poor., You reap what you sow., Do what is just, even when it costs you., Give full measure: deal honestly in trade.

  • Desire, contentment and simplicity

    Give thanks., Craving is the root of suffering., Seek the middle way; avoid extremes., Happiness is reality minus expectations., Keep it simple., More choice does not always mean more happiness.

  • Family, friends and community
    5 maxims

    Seek counsel: don't decide big things alone., Welcome the stranger., Honour your father and your mother., Leave things better than you found them., Choose your companions wisely.

  • Worry, stillness and the present moment
    4 maxims

    Make time for stillness., Everything passes; hold things lightly., Be present: the here and now is where life happens., Your thoughts shape your life.

  • Humility and self-knowledge
    5 maxims

    Look to your own faults before judging others., Be humble; pride goes before a fall., Admit your mistakes and put them right., Know yourself., Treat yourself like someone you are responsible for helping.

  • Hardship, courage and mortality
    5 maxims

    Do what is right, even when you are afraid., Remember that you will die, and live well today., Find meaning in the struggle itself., There is a time for everything; give your days a rhythm., Life contains suffering; begin by accepting it.
