Theme

Worry, stillness and the present moment

Old traditions had a good deal to say about worry: don't borrow tomorrow's troubles, make time for stillness, remember that everything passes, and notice how much depends on the way you think. These are rules for living drawn from scripture and philosophy, not medical advice; if anxiety is overwhelming, please talk to a doctor or someone you trust.
