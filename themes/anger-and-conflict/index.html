Anger, conflict and forgiveness

How to handle anger, quarrels and hurt is one of the oldest questions in moral writing. These maxims gather what scriptures, philosophers and sages from many traditions advise: be slow to anger, answer hatred with something better than hatred, forgive, and work for peace.
